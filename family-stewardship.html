FAMILY STEWARDSHIP

A family's voice belongs
at the heart of remembrance.

Families and verified representatives can request stewardship of an existing public memorial.

How stewardship is intended to work

  1. Submit a request and identify your relationship or authority.
  2. CrowRules reviews the request using a respectful, privacy-conscious verification process.
  3. Once approved, designated family stewards can manage permitted biography, media, and contributions.
  4. CrowRules continues to operate platform security, abuse response, and legal compliance.

Stewardship does not grant access to private family archives unless the family has explicitly authorized it. Disputed requests are paused for human review.
