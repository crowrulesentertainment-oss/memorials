OUR PURPOSE

Remember with care.

A thoughtful digital space for stories, legacies, and the people who live on in memory.

Our mission

CrowRules Memorials is envisioned as a respectful place to honor lives, preserve personal histories, and give families and communities a way to remember.

Dignity and accuracy

Published memorials should be reviewed for accuracy, handled with sensitivity, and submitted by people with appropriate authorization. Sample profiles on this prototype are fictional demonstrations.

Privacy and consent

Before launch, the service needs clear privacy terms, a consent process, a way to request corrections or removal, and controls for photographs and sensitive personal information.

Accessibility

We aim to support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, responsive layouts, descriptive image text, and reduced-motion preferences.

Contact and corrections

A real public launch should provide a monitored contact address and a verified procedure for reporting errors, impersonation, or inappropriate material. No contact address is configured in this prototype.
