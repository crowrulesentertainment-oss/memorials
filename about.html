OUR PURPOSE

Remembering is an act of love.

CrowRules Memorials is a worldwide remembrance and legacy platform created by CrowRules Entertainment.

Everyone deserves to be remembered.

Fame should never determine the value of a life. A parent, neighbor, artist, teacher, veteran, friend, community leader, and historical figure all have stories worth preserving.

Our goal is to offer respectful spaces for families, friends, organizations, and communities to share memories, preserve histories, and honor lives without turning grief into a competition.

Family stewardship comes first.

Some public and historical memorials may be researched and maintained by CrowRules. Families can request verified stewardship of a loved one's memorial. Family-created memorials remain under the controls granted to their authorized managers. Platform moderation and security remain the responsibility of CrowRules.

Free remembrance at the center.

Core memorial creation and remembrance features are intended to remain accessible without charge. Privacy, consent, accuracy, accessibility, and long-term preservation guide the platform.

One company. One universe. Every life, remembered with care.
