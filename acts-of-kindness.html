What Would They Have Done?

When you're not sure where to begin, think about the qualities you remember most. What did they notice? Who did they help? What made them smile? What cause mattered to them?

  • Do one unexpected kindness for someone.
  • Help a person who needs a little extra support.
  • Give your time instead of something material.
  • Teach someone something they taught you.
  • Do something today that would have made them proud.

A Living Legacy

Remembrance does not have to stay in the past. Every kind act inspired by someone we love can become another small part of their story.

Use this space to remember not only what they did, but what their example inspires you to do next.
