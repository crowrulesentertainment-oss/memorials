# Editorial and remembrance policy\n\n- Every life deserves dignity; fame does not determine worth.\n- Do not create memorials for living people.\n- Do not publish unconfirmed death reports. Require credible sources and human review.\n- Separate documented facts from personal memories, opinions, and tributes.\n- Do not speculate about cause or circumstances of death.\n- Do not use copyrighted photographs, recordings, letters, or written works without permission, a suitable license, or a valid legal basis.\n- Do not imply family endorsement because CrowRules created or maintains a public-figure memorial.\n- Provide correction, family-stewardship, privacy, and removal request routes.\n\n## Management\nCrowRules-managed public-figure and historical memorials are editorially maintained. Verified family representatives can request stewardship. Family memorials are managed by authorized family members. Community memorials require appropriate authority and permission. Organization collections do not grant access to private family archives.\n\n## Family stewardship\nVerify identity and authority through a secure, proportionate process. Record requests and decisions. Pause disputed transfers. Transfer content-management authority without transferring platform security, moderation, or legal-compliance duties. Never expose private archive material during verification.\n
