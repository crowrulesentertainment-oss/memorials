Ways to Remember

Every memorial can grow into a living collection of memories, favorite things, stories, creative work, family history, and acts of remembrance.

❤️

Favorite Things

Capture favorite foods, restaurants, drinks, movies, TV shows, music, teams, books, colors, places, hobbies, animals, games, cars, flowers, holidays, quotes, traditions, and childhood memories.

📸

Memory Gallery

Organize photographs by childhood, school, career, family, friends, vacations, holidays, hobbies, pets, and everyday life. Add stories and captions to every image.

🎵

Their Soundtrack

Preserve favorite songs and the music connected to childhood, family, friendships, road trips, celebrations, and important moments.

🎬

Movies & TV

Create a personal watchlist of favorite movies, shows, characters, actors, directors, and the movies they loved watching with family.

🍳

Their Table

Save favorite meals, restaurants, desserts, snacks, family recipes, holiday dishes, and traditions. Preserve the recipes so future generations can cook them.

🌎

Places They Loved

Build a life map showing where they were born, grew up, lived, studied, worked, traveled, and the places that became special to them.

🐾

Four-Legged Friends

Remember dogs, cats, birds, horses, and other animals they loved, with photographs, stories, names, and special memories.

📖

Memory Wall

Invite friends and family to share a favorite memory, funny story, lesson learned, or moment they will always remember.

😂

The Funny Stuff

Preserve inside jokes, funny stories, nicknames, catchphrases, favorite jokes, funny photographs, and the moments that made everyone laugh.

💬

In Their Own Words

Collect sayings, advice, catchphrases, family expressions, nicknames, and words of wisdom they were known for.

🌱

Legacy Garden

Grow a virtual remembrance garden with flowers, trees, butterflies, stars, hearts, and candles left by visitors.

🕯️

Light a Candle

Leave a virtual candle and a short message of remembrance. Create a growing display of people who stopped to remember.

🌱

Acts of Kindness

Record something meaningful done in their honor: helping someone, volunteering, donating, planting a tree, continuing a tradition, or simply checking on someone who is lonely.

🎁

Give Back in Their Memory

Connect remembrance with charitable giving to causes, community organizations, animal rescues, scholarships, or other organizations chosen by the family.

📚

Life Story

Build a biography in chapters: beginnings, growing up, family, friends, career, adventures, what they loved, and their legacy.

🧩

Did You Know?

Share small details that make a person unforgettable: hidden talents, collections, favorite sayings, unusual stories, and things only close friends knew.

🎨

Create Something for Them

Let visitors contribute poems, drawings, paintings, songs, videos, letters, stories, and other creative works made in their honor.

✉️

Letters Never Sent

A private or family-approved space to write the things someone wishes they could still say: gratitude, love, apologies, memories, and goodbye.

🎥

Memory Theater

Preserve video messages, family interviews, celebration-of-life recordings, favorite videos, and personal stories.

🎙️

Tell Their Story

Record guided interviews with family and friends: what made them laugh, what they taught you, what you admired, and what you will miss.

🧠

What They Taught Us

Create a collection of life lessons, principles, advice, and values passed from one generation to another.

🏆

A Life Well Lived

Celebrate accomplishments big and small: raising a family, community service, military service, creative work, education, career, sports, volunteering, and personal victories.

📅

Annual Remembrance

Return every year for birthdays, anniversaries, memorial dates, new stories, new candles, and family messages.

🌌

Memorial Constellation

Turn memories, photos, stories, achievements, favorite things, and family connections into stars in a growing digital constellation.

🕰️

One More Day

Invite visitors to answer: “What would you do if you had one more day with them?” Preserve those answers as part of the story.

💜

What They Meant to Me

Visitors can tell the story from their relationship: parent, child, sibling, spouse, grandparent, friend, coworker, neighbor, mentor, or another connection.

🌳

Family Tree

Connect generations and build a lasting family history archive, with memorials and stories connected across relatives.

🎂

Remember Their Birthday

Highlight birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, weddings, family milestones, and other meaningful dates with a remembrance message.

🗓️

Memory Calendar

Create a timeline of important moments and traditions throughout the year, preserving the rhythm of a life.

🏠

The Digital Memory House

Turn the memorial into an explorable home where every room preserves a different part of the person's story.
